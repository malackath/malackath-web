## 1. Purpose

The parties will share information to evaluate or carry out work together on {{proposito}} (the "Purpose").

## 2. Confidential information

Any non-public information that one party shares with the other, by any means: product ideas, designs, code, customer data, prices, business plans, access credentials. Information is not confidential if it: (a) was already public or becomes public through no fault of the recipient; (b) was already known to the recipient; (c) is lawfully received from a third party; (d) is developed independently without using it.

## 3. Obligations

The recipient: uses the information only for the Purpose; shares it only with staff or subcontractors who need it and are bound by the same confidentiality; protects it at least as carefully as its own; and gives immediate notice of any leak.

## 4. Requests from an authority

If an authority requires disclosure, it may be made, notifying the other party beforehand where the law allows, and disclosing only what is necessary.

## 5. Return

When the Purpose ends or if the other party requests it, the information is returned or deleted, except for automatic backup copies, which remain confidential.

## 6. Term

This agreement lasts {{anios_nda}} years from signature. Trade secrets and personal data remain protected for as long as they remain so.

## 7. No other obligations

This agreement does not oblige either party to enter into a contract, nor does it grant any licence over the information. Ideas that Malackath already had or develops independently remain its own.
