## 1. What it covers

**Included in the fee:**
- Uptime monitoring of the site or app and downtime alerts.
- Security and software dependency updates.
- {{frecuencia_respaldo}} backups and restore tests {{frecuencia_prueba_respaldo}}.
- Renewal and monitoring of the domain, certificates and services held in the Client's name (the costs of those services are paid by the Client).
- **{{horas_incluidas}} hours per month** for small changes: copy, photos, prices, design tweaks, fixes, minor improvements.
- Monthly report of work done and hours used.

**Not included** (quoted separately or billed hourly):
- New features or screens, redesigns, new integrations.
- Issues caused by changes made by third parties or by the Client.
- Hosting, domain, licence, app store and advertising costs.
- Recovery from attacks or data loss originating outside Malackath's control, other than restoring the latest backup.

## 2. Hours

- Included hours **do not roll over** from one month to the next.
- Extra hours are charged at the Schedule A rate{{descuento_horas_extra_texto}}, with prior notice to the Client when a request will exceed them.

## 5. Term

Minimum term of {{meses_minimos}} months; after that it renews automatically month to month. Either party may end it with {{dias_preaviso_mantenimiento}} days' written notice. On termination, Malackath hands over access, code and the latest backup within {{dias_entrega_final}} business days.

## 6. Access

The Client owns its accounts (domain, hosting, app stores, Shopify) and gives Malackath collaborator access. Malackath does not store the Client's passwords outside a secure password manager and returns or deletes them on termination.
