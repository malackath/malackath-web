## 2. How hours are counted

- Hours are logged in increments of {{fraccion_minutos}} minutes, with date, person, task and time.
- Billable hours are actual working time: design, development, testing, meetings, project management and communication with the Client or its suppliers.
- Travel time is not charged unless the SOW states otherwise.

## 3. Estimate and cap

- Every estimate is indicative, not a fixed price.
- Malackath notifies the Client in writing on reaching **80%** of the estimated hours, with what remains and how much more it would take.
- Hours will not exceed the SOW **cap** without the Client's written approval (an email is enough).

## 4. Reporting and invoicing

- Invoicing is **monthly**, in arrears, with the detailed hours for the month.
- The Client has {{dias_objecion_horas}} days to dispute a specific line in writing. Anything not disputed is deemed approved and the rest of the invoice is paid as usual.

## 5. Expenses and materials

Licences, hosting, domains, stock images, third-party services, printing and travel requested by the Client are charged at cost {{recargo_gastos_texto}}, with receipts, or are contracted by the Client in its own name. No expense above {{moneda}} {{gasto_sin_aprobacion}} is incurred without prior approval.
