## 1. Purpose

1.1. This agreement sets the general terms under which Malackath provides the Client with design, software development, websites, applications, brand identity and related services (the "Services").

1.2. Each specific engagement is described in a **Statement of Work (SOW)** signed by both parties. An SOW may be charged at a fixed price or by the hour (**Time & Materials**, Schedule A). Recurring maintenance is governed by a separate **Maintenance Agreement**.

1.3. If an SOW conflicts with this agreement, the SOW prevails only for that engagement and only where it expressly says so.

## 2. Way of working

2.1. Malackath has technical autonomy: it chooses tools, languages, infrastructure providers and methods, unless the SOW states otherwise.

2.2. The Client appoints a contact person with decision-making authority, delivers the requested content, access and approvals on time, and answers queries within {{plazo_respuesta_cliente_dias}} business days. Delays by the Client extend the deadlines by the same amount of time.

2.3. Valid notices are sent by email to the addresses at the end of this agreement. WhatsApp messages may be used for coordination, but approvals and scope changes must be in writing (email or signed document).

## 3. Scope changes

Any change to the scope, timeline or price of an SOW is documented in a **Change Order** signed by both parties before that work begins. Without a signed Change Order, Malackath is not obliged to make the change.

## 4. Price and payment

Price, currency and payment method are set in each SOW and in the **Payment Terms** (common schedule), which form part of this agreement.

## 5. Acceptance of deliverables

5.1. On delivery, Malackath notifies the Client in writing. The Client has {{plazo_aceptacion_dias}} business days to approve or to send specific comments on any non-compliance with the SOW.

5.2. If the Client does not respond within that period, or uses the deliverable in production, the deliverable is deemed accepted.

5.3. Malackath corrects at no cost anything that does not comply with the SOW. New requests go through a Change Order.

## 6. Intellectual property

6.1. **Client work.** Once each SOW has been paid in full, the Client becomes the owner of the economic rights in the deliverables made specifically for it (final designs, project-specific code, copy), to use without limit of time or territory.

6.2. **Malackath tools.** Malackath keeps ownership of its tools, libraries, templates, components and prior or generic know-how. Where these are part of a deliverable, the Client receives a perpetual, non-exclusive licence, at no extra cost, to use them within that deliverable.

6.3. **Third parties.** Fonts, stock images, open-source software and third-party services are governed by their own licences. Licence costs are borne by the Client unless the SOW states otherwise.

6.4. **Discarded drafts.** Proposals not chosen remain the property of Malackath.

6.5. **Portfolio.** Malackath may show the finished work in its portfolio and social media, unless the Client forbids it in writing or a signed NDA prevents it. No confidential information will be shown.

## 7. Confidentiality

Each party keeps confidential the non-public information it receives from the other, during the agreement and for {{anios_confidencialidad}} years afterwards. This does not apply to information that was already public, that the party already knew, or that an authority requires. If a signed NDA exists, it supplements this clause.

## 8. Personal data

If Malackath processes personal data on behalf of the Client, it does so only on the Client's instructions and in accordance with {{ley_datos_personales}} *(e.g.: Law No. 18,331 in Uruguay, LGPD in Brazil)* and any other applicable law. The Client is the data controller. *[Review with a lawyer if the project handles sensitive data.]*

## 9. Warranty

9.1. Malackath fixes at no cost any functional errors the Client reports within {{dias_garantia}} days after acceptance of each deliverable, provided the deliverable has not been modified by third parties.

9.2. Outside that warranty, Malackath does not guarantee that the software will run without interruption or remain compatible with future third-party changes (browsers, app stores, APIs, hosting providers).

## 10. Liability

10.1. Malackath's total liability for any claim related to this agreement is limited to the amount actually paid by the Client in the {{meses_tope_responsabilidad}} months before the event giving rise to the claim.

10.2. Neither party is liable for loss of profits, loss of data, loss of opportunity or indirect damages, except in cases of wilful misconduct or gross negligence.

10.3. The Client is responsible for the content it provides (copy, images, trademarks) and warrants that it has the right to use it.

## 11. Relationship between the parties

The parties are independent. There is no employment relationship, partnership or agency. Each party is responsible for its own taxes, social security contributions and staff. Malackath may subcontract part of the Services and is responsible for its subcontractors' work.

## 12. Non-solicitation

During the agreement and for {{meses_no_captacion}} months afterwards, the Client will not directly hire people who have worked on its projects on behalf of Malackath without written agreement. *[Optional; delete if not applicable.]*

## 13. Term and termination

13.1. This agreement takes effect on signature and continues indefinitely while any SOW or maintenance agreement is in force.

13.2. Either party may terminate it with {{dias_preaviso}} days' written notice. The Client pays for work done up to the termination date and for expenses already committed.

13.3. Either party may terminate it immediately if the other commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within {{dias_subsanar}} days of written notice.

13.4. If the Client does not pay, Malackath may suspend the Services after giving {{dias_aviso_suspension}} days' notice.

13.5. On termination, Malackath hands over everything paid for (files, code, access) within {{dias_entrega_final}} business days. Clauses 6, 7, 8 and 10 survive termination.

## 14. Force majeure

Neither party is liable for delays caused by events beyond its reasonable control (third-party service outages, disasters, government measures). Deadlines are extended for as long as the event lasts.
