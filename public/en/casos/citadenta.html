The vision

A RECEPTIONIST THAT ANSWERS WHATSAPP EVEN WHEN THE CLINIC IS CLOSED

It answers patients on WhatsApp at any hour, books them into the clinic's Google Calendar and tells the dentist what matters. It uses the same WhatsApp number as always: nothing to migrate.

Product architecture

THREE PILLARS: ANSWER, BOOK AND RECOVER

PILLAR 01 forum

Answers at any hour

It answers questions, books, reschedules and cancels appointments in warm, brief Rioplatense Spanish. It understands voice notes and, if it doesn't know something, it asks the clinic instead of making it up.

Emergencies Severe pain, swelling or a broken tooth: it offers the first free slot and alerts the dentist
PILLAR 02 event_available

Booked in your Google Calendar

It checks real free slots, offers three options with buttons and books the appointment. The day before it sends a reminder to confirm or change the time and, once confirmed, the clinic's location.

Record Patient details, useful notes, their appointments and the whole conversation
PILLAR 03 hourglass_top

A waiting list that works on its own

When someone cancels, it finds who to give that slot to, offers it on WhatsApp and, if there's no reply, moves to the next. It also sends the six-month check-up reminder.

Report Every month: appointments booked, emergencies and slots recovered
