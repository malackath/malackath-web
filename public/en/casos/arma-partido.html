Diagnosis & Friction

THE CHAOS OF WHATSAPP GROUPS AND EMPTY COURTS

Setting up a padel match depended on WhatsApp groups: finding four players of a similar level, agreeing on a time and getting a court, all by hand.

group_off

Mismatched levels

A 3rd-category player with a 6th-category one makes for lopsided matches. Chat groups have no way to pair people by level.

cancel_schedule_send

Last-minute cancellations

When someone drops out at the last minute, the organizer has to find a replacement or lose the court.

payments

Manual payments

One player books and then has to collect from the other three, with transfers and receipts sent by message.

stadium

Empty slots at clubs

Clubs have empty slots and no direct way to let the players who could use them know.

Product architecture

THREE PILLARS: CATEGORY, MATCHMAKING AND CLUB MANAGEMENT

PILLAR 01 analytics

One account, one category

Every player has a single account and one category, from 1st to 8th, valid at every club on the platform. No decimal ratings: just the category, like on court.

What's taken into account Category · Weekly availability · Friends
PILLAR 02 radar

Matchmaking and alerts

Players enter when they can play and the app suggests 2-vs-2 matches with people in their category. All four confirm within a deadline and, if someone drops out, the next person on the waitlist gets in. Alerts arrive on the phone.

Format 2 vs 2 · Waitlist · Phone alerts
PILLAR 03 sync_saved_locally

An app for each club

Each club gets its own branded app, an admin dashboard and a front-desk screen with the day's court grid. Bookings, tournaments with brackets and rankings on the same platform.

Payment At the club · Mercado Pago, coming soon
