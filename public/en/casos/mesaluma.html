The vision

THE EXPERIENCE STARTS BEFORE THE TABLE

Planned bookings, pre-orders and floor-to-kitchen coordination for table-service restaurants. Guests book from their phone and the kitchen knows what's coming before the table arrives.

Diagnosis & Friction

THE KITCHEN FINDS OUT WHEN THE TABLE IS ALREADY SEATED

In a table-service restaurant, bookings, floor and kitchen usually work separately: the kitchen only learns the order and allergies once the guest has arrived.

event_note

Bookings without context

You know how many people are coming, but not what they want or can't eat.

warning

Allergies by word of mouth

An allergy mentioned to the waiter may not reach the kitchen correctly.

table_restaurant

Floor in the dark

Tables, delays, waitlist and no-shows are handled on paper or from memory.

soup_kitchen

No heads-up for the kitchen

Without knowing what's coming, the kitchen can't prepare service in advance.

Product architecture

THREE PILLARS: BOOKING, FLOOR AND KITCHEN

PILLAR 01 restaurant_menu

Booking with pre-order

Guests book in 4 steps from their phone, without an account: table and time, how they want to experience dinner, allergies, and guarantee or deposit.

Options Table only · Pre-selection · Advance order · Special experience
PILLAR 02 table_bar

Real-time floor

Floor plan with live table status, joining and splitting tables, walk-ins, waitlist and delay alerts for hosts and waiters.

Devices Tablet or phone · PIN-protected stations
PILLAR 03 skillet

A kitchen that plans ahead

Queue by station with a timer per order. Allergies arrive as an alert, without the guest's personal data.

From the manual “The kitchen already knows there's a coeliac guest at table 12”
