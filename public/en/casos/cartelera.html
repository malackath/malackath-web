The vision

EVERYTHING HAPPENING IN URUGUAY, IN ONE PLACE

An events guide for all 19 departments with the real price of each ticket and who sells it. Our own engine gathers events from many sources and only publishes what is confirmed.

Diagnosis & Friction

THE CULTURAL AGENDA IS SPREAD ACROSS TOO MANY PLACES

The country's events are scattered across ticketing sites, guides, venues, local governments and media. To know what's on this weekend you have to check many pages.

hub

Scattered sources

Each ticketing site and venue publishes its own events, in its own format.

sell

Hidden prices

The real price often only shows at the end of checkout, or not at all.

map

The whole country

Guides tend to focus on Montevideo; the other departments are left out.

content_copy

Duplicate events

The same event shows up in several sources with different details.

Product architecture

THREE PILLARS: SEARCH ENGINE, REVIEW AND GUIDE

PILLAR 01 manage_search

Our own search engine

It crawls 15 sources, groups listings of the same event and only delivers new ones. It respects robots.txt, goes slowly and never bypasses logins or captchas.

Rules Uruguay only · Official time · Official image
PILLAR 02 fact_check

Review before publishing

What an official source confirms is published automatically; the rest goes through a review panel first.

Criteria No made-up data · Cancelled events removed
PILLAR 03 calendar_month

A guide for the public

List, map, mixed and calendar views, with filters, “Near me”, favorites and export to the phone's calendar.

Languages Spanish · English · Portuguese
