## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-impact digital craftsmanship at the nexus of dark-mode luxury, editorial brutalism, and hyper-kinetic agency performance. Built for an award-winning creative studio, the interface communicates technical mastery, uncompromising taste, and avant-garde confidence. 

The aesthetic marries deep obsidian depths with razor-sharp structural grids and explosive electric acid highlights. Micro-interactions should feel tactile, weighted, and magnetic—creating a visceral sense of dimension through liquid transitions, frosted optical clarity, and hairline iridescent boundaries.

## Colors

The palette revolves around deep pitch-black voids, technical charcoals, and razor-sharp spectral accents:

- **Primary (`#D4FF00` - Electric Acid Lime):** Used exclusively for high-priority focal points, active states, key interactive moments, and authoritative callouts.
- **Secondary (`#8B5CF6` - Iridescent Chrome Violet):** Supplies depth and spectral sheen; used in iridescent gradient edges, blurred chromatic flares, and secondary media tags.
- **Tertiary (`#00F0FF` - Cyan Ray):** A supplementary chromatic accent reserved for data visualization highlights, technical indicators, and hover-flare micro-gradients.
- **Neutral Surface Palette:**
  - Base Obsidian Canvas: `#08080A`
  - Elevated Container / Charcoal Surface: `#121217`
  - Subtle Border Line: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
  - Active / Hover Rim: `rgba(212, 255, 0, 0.35)`
- **Typography Tone:**
  - High-Contrast Primary: `#FFFFFF`
  - Muted Technical Secondary: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)`
  - Subdued Timestamp / Metadata: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.40)`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy relies on a dynamic tension between three specialized families:

- **Syne (Headlines & Editorial Statements):** Sculptural, bold, and unapologetically artistic. Display styles prioritize tight leading and negative tracking to form structural graphic lockups.
- **DM Sans (Body Copy & Narrative Descriptions):** High legibility, neutral geometry, and open apertures to allow complex agency case study narratives to breathe without visual fatigue.
- **Space Mono (Metadata, Indicators, Tags, & Indexes):** Industrial and precise. Rendered in uppercase with generous letter spacing to provide brutalist framing against rich visual imagery.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored by an asymmetric 12-column grid system calibrated for cinematic storytelling and dynamic editorial cadence:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with generous outer margins (`4rem`) and spacious gutters (`1.5rem`). Content alternates between tightly locked modules and full-bleed media sections.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns with compressed outer margins (`2rem`) and `1.25rem` gutters. Multi-column showcase cards compress into alternating 4-column blocks.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with tight margins (`1.25rem`) and `1rem` gutters. Stacks collapse into single-column vertical rivers while preserving high-contrast text sizing.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Generous section spacing (`space-2xl` and `space-3xl`) isolates project case studies, turning scroll interaction into a gallery-like exhibition experience.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on traditional muddy drop shadows; instead, it is achieved through optical filtration, luminous ambient backdrops, and spectral hairline borders:

- **Glassmorphic Substrates:** Frosted panels use backdrop blur (`24px` to `40px`) paired with translucent surface fills (`rgba(18, 18, 23, 0.70)`).
- **Hairline Precision Borders:** Cards and containers feature a crisp 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) that refracts into an acid green or chromatic violet edge upon hover or selection.
- **Atmospheric Glows:** Floating elements project ultra-diffused, colored optical flares (`0px 0px 48px rgba(212, 255, 0, 0.15)` or `0px 0px 64px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.18)`), creating a neon-lit darkroom ambience without muddying content readability.

## Shapes

The geometric identity balances brutalist discipline with ergonomic tactile softness:

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px):** Applied to technical chips, data cells, code blocks, and primary inputs to retain an architectural, razor-sharp discipline.
- **Medium / Large Containers (0.5rem to 0.75rem / 8px to 12px):** Applied to project preview cards, modal sheets, and showcase frames.
- **Full Pill Exceptions:** Buttons, category badges, and floating navigation pills deliberately employ a `9999px` full-rounded contour to create high visual contrast against rectilinear layout grids.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic CTA:** Fully rounded pill container filled with `#D4FF00` text in obsidian `#08080A` (`Space Mono`, bold uppercase). On hover, scales by 1.02x with an ambient lime aura and magnetic cursor tracking.
- **Secondary Ghost Button:** Translucent frosted black surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`) with a 1px white border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`) and white text. Hovers trigger an iridescent gradient border transition to `#8B5CF6`.

### Project Showcase Cards
- Constructed with charcoal glass (`rgba(18, 18, 23, 0.75)`) and 0.5rem corner radiuses. Feature 16:9 or 4:5 media viewports overlaid with subtle film-grain texture.
- Title transitions from stark white to neon lime on hover, accompanied by a 45-degree arrow icon that translates diagonally.

### Pill Badges & Metadata Chips
- Compact, fully rounded pill tags using `label-sm` in `Space Mono`.
- Outlined with hairline borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`) and backdropped with low-opacity charcoal (`rgba(18, 18, 23, 0.6)`). Active filter chips invert to solid acid lime with pitch-black text.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Understated technical fields with an obsidian-well background (`#0B0B0E`) and subtle 1px border.
- Floating monospaced labels (`Space Mono`). Focusing pulses the border into `#D4FF00` alongside an atmospheric glow halo.

### Interactive Micro-Elements
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Minimalist geometric frames (`16px x 16px`) with an electric acid check fill or glowing inner core upon selection.
- **Custom Cursor:** Magnetic ring that snaps and expands over interactive links, converting into a blended difference-mode indicator over white assets.
